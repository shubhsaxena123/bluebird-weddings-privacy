Privacy Policy

Bluebird Weddings app · Effective date: 8 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Bluebird Weddings app (“the app”) collects, why, who can see it, and what choices you have. The app is provided by Bluebird Events Pvt. Ltd. (“Bluebird”, “we”, “us”), CT-2, 202, Taj Nagri, Amar Lok Colony, Phase II, Tajganj, Agra, Uttar Pradesh 282001, India. Questions about this policy: bluebirdeventsindia@gmail.com.

1. What the app is for

The app helps guests and organisers of a wedding. A guest joins with a personal invite code, checks in (arrival, travel, who is coming), and then sees the wedding’s functions, schedule, announcements, venue and stay details, games, and a help desk. Organisers manage guests, rooms, travel and announcements.

2. Information we collect

InformationWhen and why
Name, relation to the couple, side (bride’s or groom’s), mobile numberAt check-in. Lets the organisers know who is coming and reach you during the wedding.
Arrival and departure details (dates, times, car, train, flight or bus number, whether a driver is needed)So the family and the Bluebird team can plan pickups, drops and rooms.
Group details (number of adults, children and their ages, extra bed)To plan rooms and beds.
ID photo of each person in your group (Aadhaar, passport or other ID), or a note that you will show it at the hotel deskOnly if you choose to add it, because hotels require ID at check-in. Stored privately; visible only to the organisers of your wedding.
Messages and answers (help-desk messages, blessings, game answers and scores)To deliver them and run the games.
Invite code and an anonymous device sign-inThe app signs your phone in quietly without an email or password, so you only see your own wedding. Organisers who use a username and password (the Bluebird team) sign in with those.

What we do not collect. The app does not use your location, contacts or microphone. The camera and photo gallery are used only when you choose to add an ID photo. There is no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking, and we do not sell your information.

3. Who can see your information

  • Other guests can see your name and the blessings you post, and your points in the games. They cannot see your phone number, travel or ID.
  • Family members in your group can see the group’s shared arrival details.
  • Organisers of the wedding (family helpers and the Bluebird team) can see every guest’s check-in details, phone number and ID photos.

Access is enforced in our database, so people outside a wedding cannot read its guest list, IDs or messages.

5. How long we keep information

Wedding data is kept until the wedding is over and the organisers close it. We delete ID photos and phone numbers within 60 days after the wedding unless the family asks us to keep them for longer.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Leave a wedding from More → Leave this wedding. This signs you out of the app on your phone; your check-in stays with the organisers until you ask for deletion.
  • Delete your data. Email bluebirdeventsindia@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my data” and the name and phone number you checked in with. We will delete your check-in, ID photos and messages within 30 days and confirm by email.
  • Ask for a copy of your information, or ask us to correct it, at the same address.

7. Children

The app is meant for adults. A family’s lead may enter the names and ages of children travelling with them; we use these only for room planning. We do not knowingly collect information directly from children.

8. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections, ID photos are stored privately, and access rules in our database limit who can read what. Invite codes work once, and repeated wrong codes or passwords are slowed down. No system is perfectly secure.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
